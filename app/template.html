How the numbers were made

Class times, rooms and seat caps come from the published Semester A 2026/27 timetable (1,585 courses, 5,056 sections). The students are simulated: each major's Year 1 study plan and intake estimate, scaled so every course fits its real seats. GE1601 is online and Gateway electives are not modelled.

First come, first served: students register one at a time in random order, and each takes the timetable with the fewest gaps among the seats still open. The planner assigns everyone together, in groups of up to 12 students from the same major, and also keeps the busiest campus hour as low as it can.

Gap hours are counted for every teaching week (weeks 1 to 13) and averaged.
